*Originally written September 28, 2026; published November 24, 2026.*

I've been doing a random assortment of things lately and I wanted to write them down as I'm trying to break out of my shell a bit more. This can also be a little point in time writing of what life has been like lately.

In June of this year I moved to Davis, California to be closer to my partner's family. It has been a blessing being able to get all the help that we had been starving for in Minnesota. I will forever love Minnesota and miss our home there, but have found enough positives since moving to Davis that it all seems to be the right choice.

I recently started attending meetups of the Davis Movie Club that meets at random times at random intervals for movies I would never watch. It has been nothing but great, have gotten to meet folks that are both new to Davis, or have been established for a bit, and they all find movies interesting enough that it self selects for some pretty chill foolks. We've seen Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma, and Buddy, both movies that I would never watch as I find the 'horror' (very loosely) films, and these two changed my mined on what you can do in that genre. I'm looking forward to seeing more films with the club.

Through that group I found a book club and speed listened to The Picture of Dorian Gray, which I hadn't read before, but some scenes just really resonated with my more insufferable attitudes in my early 20s. Met some good folks there as well, and looking forward to the next book Lilith on the theme of mythology.
